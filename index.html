Who we are

Leah Social Media & Graphic Design Co-Founders Agustin and Miranda Wainberg are dedicated to creating authentic, emotional connections between you and your audience.

Agustin, originally from Buenos Aires, Argentina, brings years of experience in music, ministry, and graphic design. His passion for creativity and connecting with people shines through in every project.

Miranda, a Houston native, has 11 years of experience in TV journalism, public relations, and social media. She excels in helping others tell their stories in fresh and innovative ways.

Outside of work, they are passionate about their two children and actively serving their local congregation.
